## 0. Strategic verdict (read this first)

**Be honest about "stacking real money" in a 5-week window.** Starting from zero, you almost
certainly will *not* cross YouTube/TikTok ad-revenue thresholds before the final. Platform ad
money is a *later* payoff. So the real game during the tournament is:

1. **Use the World Cup as an audience-acquisition accelerant** — the cheapest, fastest follower
   growth you'll ever get, because search/algorithm demand is about to spike for ~5 weeks.
2. **Layer money you CAN earn from day one on top of that audience:** affiliate links, TikTok
   LIVE gifts, and small sponsorships — none of which require hitting a follower threshold.
3. **Bank the audience asset** so ad revenue, memberships, and bigger sponsorships compound for
   months *after* the trophy is lifted.

**The single biggest risk that kills football channels: FIFA/broadcaster copyright.** Do not build
on match footage or broadcast commentary. Build on your own voice, stats, news, and graphics.
(Section 2 is the most important in this doc.)

## 2. ⚠️ The legal guardrails (the #1 thing that kills football channels)

**How aggressive is enforcement?** Extremely. FIFA pressures Google/YouTube to **proactively scrub
protected World Cup footage via Content ID** — often before anyone watches it. Broadcasters (Fox,
Telemundo, BBC, beIN, etc.) pay fortunes for exclusivity and defend it hard.

**What's protected (do NOT reuse):**
- Match footage / highlights / any broadcast video.
- **Broadcast commentary is *separately* copyrighted** — even audio from a stream is infringing.
- The "FIFA World Cup" wordmark, official emblem, mascot, trophy imagery, and official graphics
  are trademarked — risky in names, logos, and thumbnails.

**The fair-use trap:** "Fair use" (US) / "fair dealing" (UK/Nigeria) *can* cover criticism,
commentary, news reporting, and teaching — **but it is narrow and fact-specific, decided case by
case, and is a defense you raise *after* you've been struck, not a shield that prevents the
strike.** "I added commentary" or "no copyright intended" does **not** make reused footage safe.
Content ID is automated and doesn't care about your disclaimer. Three strikes = channel terminated.

**✅ What a faceless creator CAN safely build with:**
- **Your own original voiceover** (analysis, news, hot takes, scripts).
- **Statistics & data** (facts aren't copyrightable) — stat cards, xG, head-to-head, records.
- **News reporting/aggregation** in your own words (cite sources; don't copy article text).
- **Your own motion graphics, animations, tactics-board diagrams, charts.**
- **Properly licensed stills/B-roll** (see below).

**✅ Where to legally source visuals:**
- Free/commercial-use libraries: **Pexels, Pixabay, Unsplash** (photos + some video). Check each
  asset's license; avoid anything showing logos/branding you can't use.
- Editorial photo agencies (Getty/AP) require a **paid editorial license** — accurate but not free.
- Official **press/media images** sometimes have usage terms — read them; most bar commercial reuse.
- **Make your own:** Canva graphics, AI-generated backgrounds, animated stat cards. This is the
  safest, most scalable, most "ownable" look — and what successful faceless channels lean on.

**✅ Music for short-form:**
- Use each platform's **in-app commercial/Sounds library** (TikTok Commercial Music Library,
  Instagram, YouTube Audio Library). ⚠️ Note: **business/brand accounts on TikTok/IG are limited to
  the *commercial* music library** — popular chart songs are often *not* cleared for business use.
- Or use **royalty-free** music (YouTube Audio Library, Pixabay Music) for cross-platform safety.
- Never rip commercial songs into a monetized video.

**How real football channels stay monetized without footage:** voiceover news recaps over stills/
graphics, stat-and-ranking animations, tier lists, tactics-board breakdowns, transfer/rumor
explainers, "player journey" mini-docs, and AI/animated visuals. No broadcast video required.

## 4. Growth playbook — faceless short-form that the algorithm rewards

**Hooks (first 1–3 seconds decide everything):**
- Lead with the payoff/controversy: *"This is why [team] is going OUT in the group stage."*
- Number/curiosity: *"3 stats that prove Messi isn't done."*
- Pattern interrupt + on-screen text that repeats the spoken hook.
- Question the viewer must answer: *"Can the USA actually win their group? Watch this."*

**Length:** Keep most clips tight (**20–40s**) for retention — **but** to qualify for TikTok Creator
Rewards you need **>1 minute**, so make some "value" videos 60–90s. On YouTube, do **both** Shorts
(reach) and **long-form 6–12 min** analysis (ad money).

**Cadence during the tournament:** This is a sprint. Aim **2–4 short clips/day** at peak. Batch
evergreen pieces (team previews, player stories) *now*; post **reactive** clips within
**30–90 min of a final whistle** to ride the search/algorithm spike. Best posting times: right
before kickoff (previews) and right after the whistle (reactions).

**Discoverability / SEO:**
- Put the **keyword in the spoken hook, the on-screen text, the caption, AND the file/title**
  (TikTok and YouTube are search engines).
- Hashtags: 3–5 focused — `#WorldCup2026 #[team] #[player] #football/#soccer` + one niche tag.
- Ride **trending audio** (from the commercial library) and **breaking news** the moment it drops.
- Reaction/news content gets a free reach boost during live events — be fast.

**Proven faceless formats (none need match footage):**
- News-recap voiceover over stills/graphics ("Today at the World Cup in 60 seconds").
- Stat cards & ranking animations ("Top 5 …", tier lists).
- Tactics-board breakdowns (arrows/diagrams over a pitch graphic).
- Hot-take / debate scripts ("England's biggest weakness is…").
- "What if" / scenario and player-journey mini-stories.
- Predictions & bracket content (high engagement, drives saves/shares).

**Channels to study (model the format, don't copy):** faceless/graphics-led football pages on
TikTok & YouTube that run stat-card edits, tier lists, and voiceover recaps — note their hook
structure, caption style, and posting rhythm.

**Mistakes that kill reach:** reposting other people's clips (copyright + shadowban), watermarks
from other apps, slow uploads after matches, inconsistent posting, no hook, walls of hashtags.

## 5. Free tool stack + "create once → repurpose everywhere" workflow

| Job | Tool | Notes / ⚠️ licensing traps |
|---|---|---|
| Scripting & ideas | ChatGPT/Claude + Google Trends, TikTok Creative Center, YouTube autocomplete | Free. Research trending queries daily. |
| **AI voiceover** | **CapCut built-in TTS** (free, fine for commercial use) or **Microsoft Edge "Read Aloud"/Azure neural voices** (free) | ⚠️ **ElevenLabs FREE tier = NO commercial rights + forced "elevenlabs.io" attribution. You may NOT monetize it.** You need at least the **$5/mo Starter** plan for commercial rights. Use CapCut/Edge TTS while bootstrapping. |
| Editing | **CapCut** (free) or **DaVinci Resolve** (free, more powerful) | CapCut = fastest for short-form templates + auto-captions. |
| Captions | CapCut auto-captions | Free, accurate; captions boost retention. |
| Graphics/thumbnails | **Canva free** | Commercial use OK for your designs; some elements are Pro-only — swap them out. |
| Aspect-ratio repurpose | CapCut auto-reframe / Opus Clip (free tier) | One master → **9:16** (TikTok/Reels/Shorts) + **16:9** (YouTube). |
| Scheduling / cross-post | **Buffer free** (3 channels, supports TikTok/IG/YouTube Shorts/X) or **Metricool free** (1 brand, 50 posts/mo, good analytics, TikTok) | Plus native schedulers: Meta Business Suite (IG), YouTube Studio, TikTok Studio. |

**Realistic solo per-matchday routine (~2–3 hrs):**
1. **Pre-match (15 min):** check fixtures/news → write 1 preview hook + script.
2. **Produce (45 min):** TTS voiceover → drop stat cards/stills in CapCut → auto-caption → export
   master.
3. **Reframe & repurpose (20 min):** export 9:16 + 16:9; tweak captions/hashtags per platform.
4. **Post-whistle (45 min):** record a reaction script while it's hot → fast edit → publish within
   60–90 min to every platform.
5. **Schedule (15 min):** queue tomorrow's evergreen clip in Buffer/Metricool.
6. **Engage (15 min):** reply to comments in the first hour (algorithm loves this).

## 6. Launch plan — 5 days to kickoff + Week 1

**Days 1–2 (now): Set up the asset.**
- Pick a brand name (avoid "FIFA"/"World Cup" trademarks in the handle — use e.g. "TheTouchline",
  "PitchSideDaily", "[YourName] Football"). Grab the same handle on TikTok, IG, YouTube, X.
- Write keyword-rich bios + link-in-bio (Linktree free) with your affiliate links.
- Build a Canva template kit (intro frame, stat card, lower-thirds, thumbnail) in your brand colors.
- Set up CapCut, Buffer/Metricool, Amazon Associates + 1–2 jersey/streaming affiliate accounts.

**Days 3–4: Stockpile evergreen content (batch 8–12 clips).**
- "Group of Death" breakdowns (Groups I & L), host-nation previews (USA/Mexico/Canada),
  "Messi's last dance," "Mbappé vs Haaland," "Dark horses of WC2026," predictions/bracket.
- These post automatically through opening week while you handle live reactions.

**Day 5 (Jun 10): Pre-tournament surge.**
- Post your bracket prediction + "Everything you need to know about WC2026 in 60s."
- Schedule the **Jun 11 opener preview** (Mexico vs South Africa) to drop in the morning.

**Week 1 (Jun 11–17): Establish the rhythm.**
- 2–4 clips/day: 1 pre-match preview + 1–2 post-match reactions + 1 evergreen.
- One long-form YouTube video every 2–3 days (8–12 min group-stage round-up).
- Go TikTok LIVE for a watch-along of a marquee match (your commentary only — no broadcast on screen).
- Double down on whatever format gets your first breakout; kill what flops.
