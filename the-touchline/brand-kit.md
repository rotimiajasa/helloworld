# 🎨 The Touchline — Brand Kit

## Positioning
**The Touchline** is a faceless football desk that makes the World Cup make sense — fast news, sharp tactical breakdowns, and instant reactions, every day. Premium but accessible; the friend who actually knows football.

- **Tagline:** *Football, explained.*
- **Promise:** "The clearest 60 seconds on what just happened — and what it means."
- **Not affiliated with FIFA** (state this in every bio).

## Voice & tone
| Trait | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| **Sharp** | Get to the point in 3 seconds | Ramble, over-explain |
| **Confident** | Take a clear stance | Sit on the fence |
| **Credible** | Back takes with stats | Make stuff up |
| **Warm** | Sound like a smart mate | Sound like a textbook or a robot |

Energy = calm authority with edge. Think a great podcast host, not a screaming hype account.

## The 3 content pillars (your recurring series)
1. **The Brief** — fast news recaps ("Today at the World Cup in 60s").
2. **The Breakdown** — tactical analysis, explainers, rankings, player stories.
3. **The Reaction** — instant post-match takes (within 60–90 min of full time).
> Optional 4th once you've got traction: **The Verdict** — predictions & debate posts.

## Visual identity (all free tools)
**Colors**
| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#0B1F17` | Backgrounds, panels |
| Pitch Green | `#15A862` | Primary brand color |
| Chalk | `#F4F6F4` | Text on dark |
| Electric Lime (accent) | `#E8FF3A` | Hooks, key numbers, CTAs — makes text POP on short-form |

**Fonts (free on Google Fonts)**
- Display/headlines: **Anton** (bold condensed — looks like sports broadcast)
- Subheads/labels: **Oswald**
- Body/captions: **Inter**

**Logo / brand device**
Wordmark **THE TOUCHLINE** in Anton, with a thin horizontal "chalk line" running under it (the touchline is literally a line on the pitch). Simple, faceless-friendly, 2-min build in Canva.

**On-screen system (build as Canva templates once, reuse forever)**
- **Stat card:** Ink panel, lime accent bar on top, big Anton number, Oswald label.
- **Lower-third:** dark bar, lime tick, white Oswald text.
- **Hook frame:** full-bleed Ink, giant Anton headline with one lime word.
- **Series sting:** 1-sec "THE BRIEF / THE BREAKDOWN / THE REACTION" title card.
- **Watermark:** small `@thetouchline` bottom-corner on every clip.

**Thumbnail style (YouTube):** Ink background, one bold subject cutout (licensed/stock or your graphic), 3–4 huge Anton words, one lime highlight word. High contrast, readable at thumbnail size.

> Build these 5 templates in Canva **once** before launch → every future video is drag-and-drop.
