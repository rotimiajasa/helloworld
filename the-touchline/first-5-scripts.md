# 🎬 The Touchline — First 5 Scripts (ready to record)

**How to use:** Record VO with CapCut TTS or Microsoft Edge "Read Aloud" (free + commercial-safe — **not** ElevenLabs free tier). Build visuals from your Canva templates. Export a **9:16 master** for TikTok/Reels/Shorts, then a **16:9** version for YouTube. Add auto-captions in CapCut. Keep `@thetouchline` watermark on every clip.

**No match footage / no broadcast audio — ever.** Visuals = your graphics, stat cards, and licensed stills (Pexels/Pixabay/Unsplash).
